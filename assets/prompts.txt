Seashore:
A calming, immersive ambient soundscape inspired by a windswept seashore. A deep, warm drone forms the foundation, with a slow, steady rhythmic pulse that ebbs and flows like the tide. Layer organic textures of distant crashing waves, soft ocean foam, airy coastal wind, and occasional far-off seagull calls. Let the sounds gradually emerge, overlap, dissolve, and return, creating a sense of spaciousness, gentle movement, and quiet introspection. Subtle harmonic shifts and evolving resonances keep the sound alive without becoming distracting. Natural, atmospheric, meditative, and slightly melancholic, with a rich low end and delicate high-frequency detail. Seamless, continuous development with no abrupt transitions, no dominant melody, no percussion-driven beat, and no vocals.
