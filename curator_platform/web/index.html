04 / MAKE IT YOURS

Make the music repeat naturally.

A loop is a short piece of music that plays again and again, so visitors can stay with an artwork for as long as they like.

How does it work?

Choose the part you want to keep—or leave Start and End as they are to use the whole recording. We blend a little of the ending with the beginning to soften the moment it repeats.

Start with a 1-second blend. A 30-second selection becomes a 29-second loop because those seconds overlap. Listen through the repeat point a few times; a longer blend can feel softer, but may mix together notes that do not fit. Your original recording is kept.
